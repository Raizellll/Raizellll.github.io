Evidence-to-Symbol Adjudicators: Component and Directional Causal Mechanisms of LLM Choice Behavior

Sep. 2025 – May 2026

Undergraduate Researcher (B.S. Thesis) · Alex Reasoning Group, Fudan University · Advisor: Prof. Yixin Cao

Combined activation patching, low-rank projection ablation (h′ = h − QQTh), linear probing, and bootstrap confidence intervals across Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct on MMLU, ARC, OpenBookQA, CommonsenseQA, and MMLU-Pro.

Identified a reproducible group of late-layer attention heads acting as shared evidence-to-symbol adjudicators across tasks (zero of 100 random-head controls exceeded selected top-8 circuits across all six core model–dataset settings). Decomposing L24 attn_out showed orthogonal removal of the shared answer subspace recovers 47.5% (1.636 / 3.444) of full ablation drop with per-example correlation Pearson r = 0.833 (Spearman ρ = 0.785), whereas random vocabulary directions yield near-zero shifts (−0.009 / 0.003). Robustness was verified under TNEWS topic classification, forced-label accuracy checks (0.942), and sequence-cloze comparisons (4.9724 vs. 1.1175 ± 2.9002).
